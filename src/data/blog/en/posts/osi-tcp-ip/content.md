## Why use network models?

When data travels from one machine to another many steps occur. To simplify design and analysis, we split the process into layers.

## What is the OSI model?

The OSI model has seven layers, from Physical up to Application. Each layer has a specific responsibility and does not overlap with other layers.

## TCP/IP — the Internet model

TCP/IP is the practical model used by the Internet. It has four layers and merges several OSI responsibilities to simplify real-world implementation.

## Quick comparison

OSI is useful for learning and diagnosing problems.
TCP/IP is the model used in production networks.

**Conclusion:** Use OSI to understand concepts and TCP/IP to implement systems.
